No account is needed to review the app: on the sign-in screen, tap "Continue without an account".

Atala builds a running training plan from the answers given in onboarding (goal, level, running days), then guides each run.

- Location is used only while a run is being recorded, to measure distance, pace and route. The background location mode keeps the recording going when the screen is locked during a run.
- Apple Health access is optional: it imports runs recorded with other apps.
- Atala Pro is an auto-renewing subscription. The paywall appears at the end of onboarding and when opening a session after the free first week.
- The coach chat answers are generated by an AI model and are limited to training guidance.

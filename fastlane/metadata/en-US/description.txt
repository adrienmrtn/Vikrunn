Atala is a running coach in your pocket. Tell it your goal, your level and the days you can run: it builds a training plan that fits your life, then guides you through every session.

A PLAN BUILT AROUND YOU
Choose your goal: a first 5K, a 10K, a half marathon, running regularly or getting in shape. Atala builds your weeks from your current level, your race date and the time you are aiming for. Each week mixes easy runs, intervals, tempo and long runs, with strength and stretching sessions alongside.

A COACH WHO ANSWERS
Pick the coach and the tone that suit you: kind, direct or playful. Busy week, tired legs, a session you cannot make? Ask. Your coach can suggest a change to your plan, and nothing moves until you tap Apply.

GUIDED ON EVERY RUN
Start a session and Atala tracks it with GPS. You see your pace against the target for each step, and your coach tells you when to push and when to ease off. Prefer to run by feel? Start a free run, or add a treadmill run afterwards.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
A race time prediction that sharpens as you log runs, your weekly volume against the plan, your personal bests and your streak of weeks. Runs recorded with other apps can be imported from Apple Health.

FREE AND ATALA PRO
Free: the sessions of your first week, free runs with GPS, your run history, streaks and a few coach messages a day.
Atala Pro: your whole plan, your coach's voice during runs, guided strength and stretching, detailed run figures, moving a session to another day and unlimited coach chat.

Atala Pro is an auto-renewing subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

Atala gives general training guidance, not medical advice. If you have a health concern, talk to a doctor before starting a new training plan.
